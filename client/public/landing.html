Streaming apps think in tracks. Classical music doesn’t.

Spotify is built around artists, albums and songs. Classical music is built around composers and their works, and every work has dozens of recordings by different conductors, orchestras and soloists.

Vivacio catalogues the works of each composer, finds every matching recording on Spotify and groups the movements together, so you can listen to a whole symphony, concerto or opera in order.

From composer to concert hall in three steps

Vivacio plays through Spotify, so you hear the full catalogue in Spotify quality.

  1. Log in with Spotify

    Connect your Spotify Premium account. You can also browse first and log in later.

  2. Choose a composer and work

    Pick Beethoven, then Symphony No. 5, or search for any composer, work, performer or album.

  3. Compare recordings and press play

    See every recording of the work side by side and play the one you want, movement by movement.

Frequently asked questions

Is Vivacio free?
Yes. Vivacio is free and has no ads. Hosting and development are supported by listeners on Patreon.
Do I need Spotify Premium?
You can browse composers, works and recordings without an account. To play music in Vivacio, log in with a Spotify Premium account.
Does Vivacio work on my phone?
Yes. Vivacio has a layout designed for phones that opens automatically in your mobile browser.
Is Vivacio part of Spotify?
No. Vivacio is an independent project and is not associated with Spotify Technology SA. Recordings are found through the Spotify search API.
Where does the composer and work information come from?
Composer and work information is used under licence from Wikipedia, IMSLP and Open Opus. Album data and cover art are provided by Spotify.
